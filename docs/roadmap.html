Why not livestreaming

  • Outside the core job: footage, each kid's highlights, personal reel, weekly recap.
  • At least 12 person-months by my estimate, the largest on the list.
  • Its audience, grandparents and scouts, is not a current user.
  • It means live video of minors going to people outside the roster.
  • It delays the wrong-kid fix, which is what damages trust today.

What Jamal and I tell Carla

  • The tagging fix, including the exact issue her own parent reported.
  • A view-only share link, so grandparents and scouts can watch without being on the roster.
  • Livestreaming as something we are exploring with a partner, not a commitment.
  • No dates until engineering has estimated the work.

The right kid in every reel, with little extra work for coaches

Guardrails

Spec · Scope

Hold uncertain look-alike clips for the coach

Clip is taggedJersey number matched to the roster

Confidence bar80% or higher normally. 95% or higher when a look-alike number is on the roster.
→
Meets the bar: publishes to that kid's reel
Below the bar: held. Kept out of reels, the recap and thumbnails.

In scope · v1

  • Look-alike detection when a roster changes: one added digit (1/11, 4/14) or swapped digits (12/21)
  • Coach check on mobile: swipe cards, one clip at a time
  • Parent “Not my kid” button on every clip
  • Recap sends on time with confirmed clips only
  • Instrumentation for every metric

Out of scope

  • Face recognition or any biometric ID of children
  • Retraining or replacing the vision model
  • Better own-team vs opponent detection
  • More look-alike rules, such as 6/8 and 3/8
  • Notification timing, share link, editor changes, livestreaming

Thresholds are estimates, tuned during the shadow week.

Spec · Human in the loop

A person decides whenever the system is unsure

The coach decides identity

The system never chooses between two look-alike kids on its own.

Parent reports are never auto-reassigned

“Not my kid” hides the clip and sends it to the coach. A second wrong guess would put a child's footage in front of a third family.

Unreviewed clips fail safe

A held clip expires after 7 days and is never published. A missed highlight beats a wrong one.

People set the thresholds

The PM and the ML lead adjust the 80% and 95% bars weekly from audit data. They never change automatically.

Spec · Risks

Two ways this fails, and what catches each

1 · Coaches don't review held clips

Looks like: the list piles up and clips expire. Kids with look-alike numbers quietly get fewer highlights than teammates.

Caught by: expired-unreviewed rate per team; kid coverage split by look-alike vs other numbers; an alert when a team has clips unreviewed for over 48 hours.

Response: a reminder at a sensible hour; let an assistant coach or team manager review; lower the hold rate if load passes 5 per game.

2 · The tagger is confidently wrong

Looks like: an arm hides the “1” in 14, so it reads as a clean, high-confidence 4. It slips past the 95% bar, or hits a pair we did not define, such as 6/8.

Caught by: “Not my kid” reports per 1,000 clips by club and number pair; a weekly audit where a person checks 50 auto-published look-alike clips.

Response: raise the look-alike threshold or add the pair rule; set a repeatedly failing pair to “always ask the coach”.

Spec · Rollout

Shadow week, pilot, then every club

Shadow week

Rules run without holding anything. Measure how many clips would be held per game, and audit a sample to set the baseline wrong-kid rate.

Pilot

Ridgeline, Brightwater and Cobblestone, the three clubs that reported it, behind a flag.

All clubs

After 2 clean pilot weeks: primary metric down, coach load at most 5 per game, expiries 10% or less.

Rollback and dates

  • Turn the flag off if recaps send late or expiries pass 25%.
  • Dates follow engineering sizing. Nothing goes to Ridgeline until then.

Open questions

  • Does the tagger output a per-clip confidence today?
  • Who besides the head coach may review clips?
  • Can confirmed clips improve the model, given they are minors' footage?
